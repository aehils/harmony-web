# Harmony Corporation

Harmony Corporation exists to bring systems, processes and people into harmony. We build what's missing, connect what's already there, and help organisations work as one.

This repository holds the company website.

## What the company is set up to do

**Technology and digital transformation.** Designing, building and maintaining bespoke software, digital platforms and IT systems, and helping private, multinational and public-sector organisations automate their processes and work more efficiently.

**Smart infrastructure and sustainable energy.** Designing, installing and maintaining IoT, smart-home and building-automation systems, along with energy monitoring and management solutions such as solar and battery storage. We also advise on renewable energy, compressed natural gas (CNG) and gas conversion, and power generation and distribution projects.

**Research, evaluation and sustainable-development advisory.** Conducting research, analysing data, and providing insight, policy advice and monitoring-and-evaluation services on sustainable development and on social and environmental challenges, including designing solutions and measuring their impact.

**Training and capacity building.** Running training and knowledge-transfer programmes, from digital skills to professional development and public awareness, that equip government agencies, schools, teachers and organisations with new knowledge and best practice.

## The website

A single static page: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js` and an `assets/` folder. There is no build step.

To preview it locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then open `http://localhost:4173`.
